One experience-agnostic way of looking at life is it being a series of choices and decisions. To make good decisions one must form a good dynamic model of the world. Forming a model includes acquiring information and making sense of it, i.e., we acquire information by sensory input or by connecting dots and making revelations through the already known.

Curiosity today is generally rewarded, thanks to language, internet, AI, and people who post on the internet. If one asks the right questions and aims at the right sources, there is a good chance they'll get some good information input. Connecting the dots part is where every individual independently creates their own mix.

Only consuming the information and not sharing insights and opinions back (especially when you have some that'd make a difference) makes you only a consumer of the system, not an "integrated asset" -- one I strive to be. Henceforth, I will use this blog as a means to express opinions and insights about AI, Technology, and beyond.
